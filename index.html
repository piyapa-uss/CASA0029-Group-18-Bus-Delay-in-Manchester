Stuck in Transit

Understanding Bus Delays in Manchester

This project investigates bus delay and reliability across Greater Manchester, asking where delays occur, how they vary across the network, and how unreliable service may shape everyday mobility.

Greater Manchester is a large city-region where mobility is uneven. This overview explains why bus reliability matters for everyday access, opportunity, and transport inequality.

Why Manchester?

Greater Manchester is a large and decentralised city-region where access to jobs, education, and services varies across neighbourhoods.

Why bus matters?

Buses support everyday mobility and often provide the most flexible public transport option for people without easy access to a car.

Impact / Uneven Mobility

This section explores how delay exposure may be experienced unevenly across Manchester and situates bus reliability within a wider socioeconomic context.

Section Framing

Bus delays are not just an inconvenience, they can have real implications for accessibility to jobs, opportunity, and essential services. This section explores how delay exposure may be unevenly distributed across Greater Manchester and how this relates to the dispersion of socioeconimic deprivation across the metropolitan area.

Key Takeaway

Delay exposure is not evenly distributed across Manchester. Some areas, especially in areas further from the city center, experience much higher average delays than others. These areas also tend to be more socioeconomically deprived, suggesting that bus delays may be exacerbating existing inequalities in accessibility and opportunity across the city.
